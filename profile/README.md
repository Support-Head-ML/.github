# TDM Research

Research on **Type-Decision Models**: models that answer structured questions
such as choosing among alternatives, judging a yes/no event, or selecting a
level on an ordered scale.

Our projects study how these models represent decisions, how their answers
relate across independently asked questions, and how to evaluate predictive
quality and consistency together.

## Projects

[Support Head](https://github.com/Support-Head-ML/support-head) investigates
whether a shared state–hypothesis scorer can make related typed decisions more
coherent while preserving predictive quality.
